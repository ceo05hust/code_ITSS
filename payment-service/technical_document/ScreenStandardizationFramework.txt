Here is the comprehensive summary of the AIMS Interface and Screen Standardization Framework in English:
Overview and Screen Layout The standardization of screen design in the AIMS web application aims to provide a consistent, responsive, and highly usable experience across different devices
.
Resolution and Adaptability: The minimum resolution is set to 1366 x 768 pixels, with the ability to adapt to mobile or tablet ratios
.
Component Placement: Menu bars must be located in a stable top area, while screen titles belong in the upper part of the page
. Primary action buttons should be consistently placed at the bottom or right side of the working area
.
Content Organization: Related information (e.g., product details, payment info) must be clearly grouped
. Text should generally be left-justified, but financial figures like totals and taxes must be right-justified in summary panels
.
UI Kit Standards The UI Kit ensures visual consistency by utilizing a set of reusable interface elements and rules
.
Typography: Inter is the primary font family, utilizing a strict size hierarchy ranging from H1 (24px bold) to helper text (12px)
.
Color Palette: The design follows a professional monochrome style featuring white backgrounds (#FFFFFF), black primary text (#000000), grey borders (#E5E5E5), and specific warning colors (#D32F2F)
.
Elements: Primary buttons feature a black background with white text, whereas secondary buttons use a white background with dark text and borders
. Input fields and buttons must have an 8px (0.5rem) border radius
. Consistent Material Icons and properly proportioned graphics are mandatory
.
Data Formatting Standardized data formats are required to improve readability and prevent user entry errors
.
Numbers & Currency: Numbers must use dot separators for thousands (e.g., 1.284.000), and currency amounts must consistently display the VND unit (e.g., 1.000 VND)
.
Dates & Identifiers: Dates follow the DD/MM/YYYY format
. Identifiers like transaction and order IDs must adhere to specific system-defined formats
.
Text & Quantity: String handling dictates that long text can be truncated with ellipses, and unsupported characters are prohibited
. Quantity fields accept only numeric values
.
Control and Navigation User interactions, navigation, and validation are governed by strict operational rules to guarantee a predictable flow
.
Input Validation: Validation must occur in a specific sequence: check if the field is empty, verify the format, and then apply business rules
.
User Flows: The system relies on predefined navigational sequences, such as the standard checkout flow (HomePage -> Product Detail -> Cart -> Delivery Info -> Review -> Complete -> Order Confirmed)
. Users must always have clear, explicit "Go Back" options
.
Interactive Controls: Search controls must support keyword inputs and price range filters
. Quantity controls allow direct input and +/- adjustments, triggering warnings if the amount exceeds available stock
. Payment screens must display total amounts, QR codes, and provide a copy-to-clipboard feature for transfer content
.
Other Standardizations
Error Handling & Feedback: Error messages must be concise, explain the problem, and guide the user on how to fix it
. Invalid fields need clear visual highlights
. Crucially, validation errors must never cause a user to lose their current progress
.
Domain Values: Terms across the system are standardized, such as limiting product types to Book, CD, DVD, and Newspaper, and standardizing statuses like "Active" or "Waiting for payment"
